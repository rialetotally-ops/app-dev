# My Favorite Series/Movie is Chainsaw Man
---
*Chainsaw Man* is an Anime series that's about a teen named **Denji** trying to live a normal life with a Devil Heart from a contract he made with *Pochita* , But everyone wants the Devil Heart for themselves. As He keeps living with the Devil Heart the worse his life spirals, Will his dream be finally achieved or will he have to lose Pochita in order to get the life he wants?
---
> Things I like about *Chainsaw Man*
1. The Music from the anime is so good, especially the opening song.
2. The animation for Chainsaw Man is really good especially in the Movie.
3. I also like the designs for each Devils in Chainsaw Man especially for Denji and Pochita when they made a contract.
